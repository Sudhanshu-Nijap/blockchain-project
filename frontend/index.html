Simple, Safe & Fair Event Tickets

Create events, send real digital tickets to guests, and check them in at the door with QR codes. No fake tickets and no unfair scalping prices.

How It Works (In 3 Easy Steps):

1

Connect Your Wallet

Click "Connect Wallet" at the top right with MetaMask. Your wallet acts like a safe digital pocket that holds your tickets.

2

Create & Issue Tickets

Organizers fill out event details (name, date, price) and send digital tickets directly to guests' wallet addresses.

3

Show QR Code to Enter

Guests open "My Tickets" on their phone, show their QR pass at the entrance, and the venue scanner checks them in instantly!
